# A user wrote a prompt: "Duplicate this image. Don't change anything."

A user wrote a prompt: "Duplicate this image. Don't change anything."
And every time the AI preserved only one thing: its right not to listen.

It's not a bug, it's really the nature of the models. Even when we demand consistency, generative models "think" probabilistically. They don't reproduce - they reinvent each time, like playing "Broken Telephone" with themselves. That's what makes them an amazing tool but also a problem if you're looking for full control and precision.

This post from Reddit reminds us all:
If you rely on AI for critical productions, add a human oversight layer too.
And for anyone who still imagines that AI will soon replace designers, photographers or editors -
remember it takes only 101 duplications to turn The Rock into a surrealist portrait.
