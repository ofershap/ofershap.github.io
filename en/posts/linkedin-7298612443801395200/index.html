**"Dad, why is nobody playing with me today?"**
My son came back from kindergarten with a dim look.

I felt my heart tighten, and I immediately wanted to tell him it was nothing, that tomorrow everything would be fine.
But then I stopped.

Because the truth? I didn't know what was really right to say.
How do you explain to a small child about social dynamics, about how one day you're the king of the sand, and the next day everyone forgets you?

So I did what I've been doing quite a lot lately -
**I asked ChatGPT.**

And the answer I got?
It was smart, gentle, and helped me phrase an answer for him that would give him strength, and not just comfort.

I shared this here and also with friends - and they started using it too and asking me for help creating content.
For one it helped to talk with his child about fears at night.
Another asked how to deal with frustration outbursts.

And that's how I realized - **we, the parents, need a place where we can get good answers, fast.**

So I built **AI Parent** - a platform that gathers smart answers for parents, combined with artificial intelligence.

🛠️ How was it built?
I used **Lovable, Cursor and ChatGPT** to create a tool that makes reliable information accessible and available to every parent, in the simplest way there is.

I created a collection of questions and answers I encountered, and a few more I asked the chat to phrase for me. And so I created a content repository (in Airtable - behind the scenes) that the site reads and displays in a form that's comfortable to read.
