# An exit in less than a month – without writing code! 🤯

An exit in less than a month – without writing code! 🤯

Imagine you create a silly but addictive game, without knowing how to program, and within less than a month you start earning tens of thousands of dollars, and then someone simply buys spaces in the game from you at crazy prices.

That's exactly what happened to @levelsio with the game fly.pieter.com.

He created the simplest flight simulator in the world, which everyone can play in the browser – and suddenly it became a hit.

He is indeed a talented developer, but what happened here is unprecedented: he developed a full multiplayer flight simulator in Python *almost without writing a single line of code* 🛩️ 
He did it using Cursor AI, while Grok 3 handled the WebSocket server. 

He created, alone and in a short time, a game where you can fly with other players in real time!

And then in his genius and following the incredible number of players who joined, 
he started making money from it:
✨ Sold ad spaces – balloons with a logo, the moon, planets
✨ Sold planes (skins for players)
✨ Found big advertisers who wanted to buy "ad space" in the virtual world

💰 And now, he updated that his revenue has passed $30,000 a month, after selling the planet Jupiter to SoftgenAI!

True, it's still not an "exit" as we know it, but relative to the investment and time - it's an event that can't be ignored.

🚀 And all this happened from a game created without programming knowledge, with tools like AI and Grok.

Is this a rare case, riding the wave of AI innovation, or the beginning of a new era?

When the internet was still in its infancy, a young student was looking for a way to fund his studies and decided to build a site with a million pixels and sell each pixel for a dollar. He got international coverage and quickly sold all the pixels. Everyone wanted to take part in that piece of history.

There are countless companies working in AI with no real market leader, Google can't keep up with indexing the companies, and they have an opportunity to stand out and become known relatively cheaply. 
Who would have thought the pixel site would come back in the form of a flight simulator 😂✈️
