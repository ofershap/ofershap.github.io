Get this! I found a crazy hack in Cursor 🤓🤘
You can add a User Rule to Cursor and tell it where your previous conversations are saved, by adding the following Rule:
```
U have access to all conversations of last month - they are stored in: ~/.cursor/projects/[YOUR-WORKSPACE-PATH]/agent-transcripts/
```
(Leave [YOUR-WORKSPACE-PATH] as is, it will work in any project you open)

From this moment you can ask it questions about past conversations and it will know and remember them!
You can also ask it to analyze the conversations and extract insights, or anything else.
Want to find conversations that disappeared? Topics you started and didn't finish? Everything is open.

Important to know: conversations are kept for 30 days, meaning there is access only to the last 30 days.

Let me know if it works for you and feel free to share what you did with it 😎
