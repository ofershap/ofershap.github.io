The Israeli company FIVERR in an unprecedented move for freelancers – it's crazy🔥
Meet Fiverr Go, the new feature that lets freelancers clone themselves - with the help of AI!

Until now, the biggest problem for independents was the time limit. Because no matter how hard we try, there are only 24 hours in a day. 
But what if your clients could generate content at your level, in your style, even while you sleep?

That's exactly what Fiverr launched 🚀 AI that generates content in your style!

They managed to respond with the speed and magic reserved for them and to connect the need and the market they serve to the technological revolution happening these days, and managed to develop a system that gives every freelancer new capabilities, and upgrade their service!

The system lets every freelancer train an artificial intelligence model that imitates their unique style - writing, design, voiceover and more.

That is, clients can use this model to generate new content, as if the freelancer had created it themselves!

🎨 Designers? Your clients will be able to generate images that feel exactly like yours.
✍️ Writers? The model will produce texts in your unique style.
🎙️ Voice artists? The AI will create new voiceover that sounds exactly like your voice.

Bottom line: work less and earn more. 

💡The service is currently available to freelancers at Level 2+ for a monthly payment of $25.

Platforms like Fiverr understand that the market is changing, freelancers can't rely only on their time, and the growing competition from AI tools demands a quick response with innovative news.

And that's what they did - it's not just a technological innovation, it's taking part in the revolution of independent work.

Micha Kaufman succeeds in surprising once again with his innovative and disruptive thinking, and in showing us that the question is not whether to use AI, but how to make AI work for us.
