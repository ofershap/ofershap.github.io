# That's it, I solved the most annoying thing in ChatGPT!

That's it, I solved the most annoying thing in ChatGPT!  
How? With a browser extension of course!

As some of you already know, I like solving problems in various systems with browser extensions (like the Jira extension I developed).
Now it's the chat's turn. Following the post by Omer Mayost on the annoying dash (the famous Em-Dash), which I experienced myself too, I realized there was no way out except developing an extension that would handle this annoying problem! 

(For anyone who missed it - this is the post: https://lnkd.in/dChAJDD5)

And I can tell you that after a short period of using the extension, it's unclear how one could manage without it 😇 

In addition, along the way, I dealt with the other problems we experience - like changing the text direction to align right (RTL), for example when English and Hebrew are mixed together

I also added a cute feature of drawing on top of images to improve our ability to explain what we want, because sometimes a picture is worth a thousand words...

The extension is free, and runs in the browser on your personal computer with no call to any server, so it's also safe to use.

Following the survey that Adir Duchan (the one from that thing) ran here not long ago about the use of AI tools, I realized there are quite a few who haven't yet moved to using Cursor, so I'm going to release a series of videos that explain how I developed this extension within a few hours over a single weekend using various AI tools - from the first lines of code to publishing in the extension store. 

Interested? I'd be glad if you comment here (and then LinkedIn will do their magic and make sure to alert you to my next posts so you don't miss them!)

You can install the extension through Google's Chrome Extensions store: 
https://lnkd.in/daRt86pa 

I'd love to hear opinions and suggestions for other things that bother you in the chat and that maybe can be solved with the extension!
