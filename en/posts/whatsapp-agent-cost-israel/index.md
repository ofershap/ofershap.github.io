# How much does a WhatsApp AI agent cost for a small business in Israel?

Installed on an existing tool, my setup fee starts at 1,800 ILS, plus a monthly subscription for the tool itself that varies by provider and chat volume.

## In depth

The cost has three parts.

**1. One-time setup.** Choosing a tool, connecting it to the business WhatsApp, writing the instructions and a knowledge base about the business, and testing. This is where I charge from 1,800 ILS.

**2. Tool subscription.** Most tools are built on a monthly subscription, sometimes with per-chat pricing. The amount depends on the provider and the volume, so I present it up front before installation instead of guessing.

**3. Maintenance.** Answers go stale: prices change, opening hours, promotions. Ongoing updates and a review of chats the agent could not answer: from 300 ILS a month.

**When is it worth it?** If you get more than a few dozen WhatsApp inquiries a week, and most are repeating questions (price, hours, availability), the agent saves time. If it is ten inquiries a week, prepared quick replies might be enough.

**When not?** When the conversations are personal and complex, like detailed medical advice. Here the agent can collect details and hand over, not answer.

When I price a WhatsApp agent installation, I separate the setup, the tool subscription, and the maintenance. That way you can see up front what you are paying for, without hiding the tool's cost inside the setup price.
