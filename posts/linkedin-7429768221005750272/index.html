I built the resource I wish I had when I started coding as a Hebrew speaker! 🇮🇱

Just published awesome-hebrew-dev: a curated list of everything a Hebrew-speaking developer needs in one place:
- Communities & meetups (JavaScript Israel, React Israel, Vue.js Israel, and more)
- Podcasts in Hebrew (עושים תוכנה, Reversim)
- Free learning platforms & courses (Codebox, Campus IL, ToCode, and others)
- Conferences (ReactNext, Reversim Summit, PyCon Israel)
- Open source projects & Hebrew NLP tools
- Job boards & tech blogs

Israel has an incredible developer ecosystem, but the resources are scattered across dozens of sites, Slack groups, Meetup pages, and YouTube channels. This list brings them all together — in Hebrew and English.

It's open source (CC0 licensed), and contributions are welcome.

Know a missing resource? Open a PR!
