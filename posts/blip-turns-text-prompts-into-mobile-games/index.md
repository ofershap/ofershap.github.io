# Blip turns text prompts into mobile games

April 14, 2025 · 1 min read

Originally posted on LinkedIn , April 14, 2025.

Blip is a platform for creating mobile games directly from your phone with AI. Instead of writing code, you describe the idea in words, and the AI turns it into a game. This approach has been called Vibe Coding.

It is not just prompt-based creation. You can open the code, change it, debug it, and refine the game as you would with a regular game engine. Blip combines that accessibility and creative spark with direct control over the result.

The founders have already released early access through TestFlight and are looking for creators, artists, and developers to try it. It feels like TikTok for game developers: anyone can create, and anyone can build.
