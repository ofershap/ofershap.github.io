# Bland's AI voice agents sound almost human

February 12, 2025 · 1 min read

Originally posted on LinkedIn , February 12, 2025.

I spoke with an AI representative, and it sounded good. Bland has developed technology for ultra-realistic phone conversations with AI. The startup reached its Series B faster than any company in history, raising $65 million within a few months.

Companies are turning to Bland because its system can replace customer service and sales call centers with conversations that feel almost human. It:

- Handles natural voice conversations and adapts to each person's speaking style.

- Runs 24/7 and can manage millions of calls at the same time.

- Connects to existing CRM, payment, and scheduling systems, then acts in real time.

You can enter your phone number on Bland's website and have the AI call you. For an Israeli number, add +972 at the beginning.

After the funding round, the first thing Bland did was buy a car shaped like a giant phone and use it to film a company ad.
