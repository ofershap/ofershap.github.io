# A cinematic cockpit prompt for any city

July 25, 2025 · 2 min read

Originally posted on LinkedIn , July 25, 2025.

I generated a cinematic view of Tel Aviv entirely from a prompt. The image shows a first-person view from a plane banking sharply over the coastline at twilight, with both hands gripping the yoke and the cockpit lit by the soft glow of its control screens. The words "Tel Aviv" flash in electric green on the dashboard.

Through the curved window, Ayalon traffic runs in blurred lines below, the Azrieli Towers glow deep purple, and Neve Tzedek catches the last golden light of sunset. It looks like a frame from a movie, but every part of it was generated from text.

Text-to-image generation is becoming more precise, bolder, and more cinematic. Instead of generic scenery or vague fantasy, a prompt can define a deep composition with dramatic lighting, clean reflections, and motion that feels like aerial photography. This prompt specifies the lighting, lens, materials, movement, and even the city name inside the image itself.

This is part of my Friday prompt series, where I share a useful prompt that can be reused with a few small changes.
