# One prompt can now generate a complete short film

March 13, 2026 · 1 min read

Originally posted on LinkedIn , March 13, 2026.

A single prompt can now become a complete cinematic short film. Characters, scenes, storyboards, music, edits, and a coherent plot are all generated together.

In one example, a creator simply typed: “What is happening in the Middle East right now?” Within moments, the system produced a polished video that looked like a professionally edited short film.

This is where generative video is going. Not random clips stitched together, but systems that can turn a simple idea into a complete visual story from beginning to end.
