Ask GEMINI from the CHROME address bar

January 15, 2025 · 1 min read

Originally posted on LinkedIn, January 15, 2025.

Type @ followed immediately by GEMINI in the CHROME address bar. It turns into a text box where you can write a question, or PROMPT, and send it to GEMINI, Google's AI engine.

There is no need to open a new tab or look for another tool. Google is making AI part of the browser and easier to use in everyday work.
