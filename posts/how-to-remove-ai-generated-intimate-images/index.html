How to remove AI-generated intimate images

August 12, 2023 · 1 min read

Originally posted on LinkedIn, August 12, 2023.

If someone used AI or Photoshop to turn your photo into a nude image, submit both the original and edited images to StopNCII.org. They will work to remove the edited image from sites across the internet. You do not need to speak directly with anyone, and your identity will remain confidential.

If the image has gone viral, notify the platform’s cybersecurity team immediately and act at once.
