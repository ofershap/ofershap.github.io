# A prompt for freezing any emoji in a cinematic ice cube

August 1, 2025 · 1 min read

Originally posted on LinkedIn , August 1, 2025.

This prompt turns any object, emoji, or word into a glossy, frozen 3D image. Picture a juicy strawberry, a bright orange, or a glowing light bulb trapped inside a completely transparent ice cube, covered by a thin layer of frost and rendered almost more realistically than the real thing.

I used the emojis 🍓🍊🍅🍐🍒🍏, but 💡, ❄️, or anything else can take their place. The setting is a dim, blurred kitchen with soft modern lighting and cool colors. Air bubbles, scratches, light refractions, and uneven textures make the ice itself part of the image.

The result looks like a minimalist ad for something that does not exist, built around transparency, texture, light, and cold.
