# An Independence Day kids' meal toy made with ChatGPT

May 3, 2025 · 1 min read

Originally posted on LinkedIn , May 3, 2025.

I used ChatGPT to imagine what it would look like if McDonald's handed out Independence Day toys with its kids' meals.

Here was the prompt:

Turn the character in the image into a small collectible toy that could be included as a gift in a McDonald's meal. Design the character as a small, shiny plastic toy standing on a simple display base. In the background, include a realistic McDonald's burger and branded meal packaging to make it look like a promotional kids' meal toy. Use soft lighting, a clean background, and bright, cheerful colors.

Happy Independence Day.
