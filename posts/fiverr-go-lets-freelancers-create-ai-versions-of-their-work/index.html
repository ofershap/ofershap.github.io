Fiverr Go lets freelancers create AI versions of their work

Original · English · العربية · Español · 简体中文

February 20, 2025 · 1 min read

Originally posted on LinkedIn, February 20, 2025.

Fiverr has launched Fiverr Go, a new service that lets freelancers replicate their work with AI.

Time has always been the main constraint for independent workers. No matter how hard you work, there are only 24 hours in a day. Fiverr Go changes that by letting clients create content at your level and in your style, even while you sleep.

Each freelancer can train an AI model to imitate their distinct style across writing, design, voiceover, and other types of work. Clients can then use that model to create new content as if the freelancer had made it personally.

  • Designers can let clients generate images that look like their work.
  • Writers can produce text in their own writing style.
  • Voice actors can generate new recordings that sound like their voice.

The result is simple: freelancers can work less and earn more. The service is currently available to Level 2+ freelancers for $25 per month.

Fiverr understood that its market was changing. Freelancers can no longer depend only on selling their time, and growing competition from AI tools demands a fast response. Fiverr connected that shift to the needs of the market it already serves and gave freelancers a new way to extend their services.

This is more than a technical feature. It gives freelancers a role in the AI-driven shift in independent work. Micha Kaufman has once again shown that the question is not whether to use AI, but how to make AI work for us.
