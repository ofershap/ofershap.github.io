# Building complete websites with AI

February 17, 2025 · 1 min read

Originally posted on LinkedIn , February 17, 2025.

The recording of my talk on building websites with AI at the Lashevet coworking space in Shefayim is now available on YouTube.

The talk is split into 45 minutes reviewing AI and different website-building tools, followed by another 45 minutes where I demonstrate LIVE with the audience. I take their ideas and turn them into complete, content-rich websites as easily as writing a message to Chat GPT.

Seeing an idea in our head take shape as something visual that we can play with, explore, and get feedback on is a new capability that has never been available to us before. Everyone should know how to use it.

“If there is one thing I want you to take away from this talk, it is that we are at the beginning of a revolution, and the only way to be part of it is to try. Almost every website tool and system you know has added AI in one form or another, alongside new websites, systems, and products built around this capability. All that is left is for you to try.”

Thank you to Dor Kochavi and the Lashevet center for hosting me and giving me the opportunity to speak.
