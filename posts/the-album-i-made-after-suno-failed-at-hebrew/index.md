# The album I made after Suno failed at Hebrew

January 19, 2025 · 1 min read

Originally posted on LinkedIn , January 19, 2025.

My band, Lorem Ipsum, has released its debut album. It was a long journey filled with creative moments, a passion for music, and a lot of love. This album is more than words and melodies. It is our story, and it touches all of our hearts.

The producer is none other than DJ SUNO, maestro of sound. We invested dozens of minutes and dozens of dollars in this album, all from the heart and soul.

The album actually started as an attempt to create songs in Hebrew with SUNO. I discovered that it could not pronounce Hebrew words properly, even after I tried adding vowel markings dozens of times in different ways. Out of that frustration, I produced the only thing that could be useful, sort of, and here we are.

The album is now available on all major streaming platforms, including Spotify . It works especially well as background music while working.
