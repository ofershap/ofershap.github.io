# GitHub output from 10 prolific developers

February 27, 2026 · 1 min read

Originally posted on LinkedIn , February 27, 2026.

I visualized the GitHub profiles of 10 developers. The numbers are wild:

- Linus Torvalds : 226K stars from 6 repos. Six. One of them is Linux.

- Sindre Sorhus : 947 projects and 989K stars. Nearly a million. One person, working full-time on open source with no employer.

- Tj Holowaychuk : 214 projects and 135K stars. He built Express.js, Koa, Commander, Mocha, Pug, and basically half of early Node.js.

- Evan You : Created Vue.js and Vite across 73 repos. Quality over quantity, redefined.

- Addy Osmani : 173 projects and 57K stars. Engineering Lead at Google Chrome. He literally wrote the book on JavaScript design patterns.

- Anthony Fu : 205 projects and 61K stars. Vitest, Slidev, UnoCSS, and dozens of Vue/Vite tools. He ships at inhuman speed.

- Michael Paulson : 191 projects and 40K stars. He went from Netflix engineer to the biggest dev content creator and open sources everything.

- Dan Abramov : 54 projects and 39K stars. He co-created Redux and React's new docs. Now he is building at Bluesky.

- Kent C. Dodds : 344 projects and 39K stars. Testing Library, Epic React, and Remix contributor.

- shadcndesign.com : 145 repos, with shadcn/ui used by half the internet. It proved that copy-paste components beat npm packages.

All of these profiles were generated from public GitHub data. To generate yours, open gitshow.dev/YOUR_USERNAME . There is no signup and no config. You can also replace github.com with gitshow.dev in any profile URL.

The project is open source .
