Alon Musk’s Neuralink Startup started recruiting volunteers to test brain implants.
Would you join?

They are seeking individuals with mobility diseases for first human clinical trial of technology that aims to use brain to control computers, and one day treat neurological disorders. In the future, you will be able to save and replay memories, You could potentially download them into a new body or into a robot body.
