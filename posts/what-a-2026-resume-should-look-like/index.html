What a 2026 résumé should look like

July 6, 2026 · 1 min read

Originally posted on LinkedIn, July 6, 2026.

Elementor had to let go of some of its best people last week as a necessary efficiency measure in a market that has changed.

I read a post by Roey Tzezana arguing that sending a résumé as a PDF is outdated in 2026. It made me think about how I would submit mine today. This is probably what it would look like. View it on a desktop if you want to see the scroll-based video background.

I built the site with Sticklight by Elementor, and originally created the video with Gemini. Sticklight includes 5 free credits, which is enough to build quite a lot.

There are also many talented former Elementor employees now looking for their next role across engineering, design, marketing, and other fields. They are exceptional people. If you have a relevant opening, send it my way and I will make the connection.
