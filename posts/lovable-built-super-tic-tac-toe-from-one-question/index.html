Lovable built Super Tic Tac Toe from one question

December 14, 2024 · 1 min read

Originally posted on LinkedIn, December 14, 2024.

I saw an Instagram reel featuring a brain-challenging game called Super Tic Tac Toe. Instead of the regular version, it has a grid of 9 smaller games. Each move inside a smaller grid determines where the opponent has to play next.

I asked the new AI website generator Lovable.dev one question: "Do you know the game Super Tic Tac Toe?" From that short prompt, it built a complete, working game. You can play it here.

I first tried the same task with Anthropic's Claude AI, but it failed. The score is 0:1, Claude AI vs Lovable AI. This is what I call a revolution.
